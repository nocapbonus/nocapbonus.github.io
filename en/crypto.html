Payment methods

On crypto casinos, you have several ways to deposit: directly in cryptocurrency, or by card through a purchase platform that converts your money into crypto. Here's an overview of the options.

Main cryptocurrencies

₿

The first and best-known cryptocurrency. It's the reference currency, accepted on virtually all crypto casinos.

Ξ

The second-largest cryptocurrency, also serving as the base for many other tokens. Widely supported on gaming platforms.

Often described as the "light and fast" version of Bitcoin, with lower transaction fees and faster confirmations.

A recent blockchain known for its speed and very low fees, making it convenient for frequent deposits and withdrawals.

Originally designed for fast money transfers between institutions, it offers near-instant, low-cost transactions.

Depositing by card on a crypto casino

You don't need to already own crypto to play on these platforms. Third-party services, called "on-ramps", let you buy crypto directly with your card, then send it to your casino account.
