How does it work?

Deposit with your skins

On most CS2 sites, you can deposit skins straight from your Steam inventory. Their value is converted into a balance on the site, which you then use to play.

Deposit with card or crypto

Depending on the site, you can also deposit by card or with cryptocurrencies. Our payment methods guide explains how it works.

Available games

Case opening (opening cases to get skins), battles (head-to-head openings against other players) or upgrade (trying to turn a skin into a higher-value one): each site has its own modes, listed on each card.

Withdrawing your winnings

You can usually withdraw as skins sent to your Steam account, or in crypto depending on the site. Check the processing times and minimum withdrawal amount.

Before you play

Skins have real money value: playing with them means gambling real money. Set a budget before you start, never chase your losses, and stop when it's no longer fun.

If you feel gambling is taking up too much space in your life, talk about it and get help. Free, confidential resources exist, such as BeGambleAware.
