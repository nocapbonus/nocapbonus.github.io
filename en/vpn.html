What is a VPN?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) is a tool that hides your real location by routing your internet connection through a server located in another country. It also encrypts your traffic for more privacy.

Why use one for online casinos?

Some platforms aren't accessible from every region of the world, often for local licensing reasons. A VPN lets you choose a server in a country where the platform is available, so you can access it normally.

💡 Always check the terms of use of the platform you're playing on: some casinos explicitly allow VPN use, others don't. That's why we show it via the "VPN allowed" badge on our cards.
