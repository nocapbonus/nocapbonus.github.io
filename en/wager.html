What is a wager?

The wager (or "betting requirement") is a condition set by an online casino before you can withdraw a bonus and the winnings from it. It's one of the most important things to understand before accepting an offer.

The simple definition

When a casino offers you a bonus (deposit, free spins, cashback...), it may require you to re-bet that amount a certain number of times before you can withdraw it. This multiplier is called the wager, usually expressed as "x" (x20, x35, x40...).

The formula

A 100% bonus with a x30 wager on a €100 deposit = €200 starting balance to bet 30 times

€200 × Wager x30 = €6,000 to bet before withdrawal

As long as you haven't bet this amount on eligible games, your winnings remain locked. This is why some offers, despite a high bonus, are actually not very appealing if the wager is too high.

What is "Wager Free"?

A Wager Free offer (or "no wager") is an offer where the multiplier required before withdrawal is simply x0. What you win or receive is directly withdrawable, with no re-betting needed.

Be careful though, it works a bit differently than you might think: with Wager Free, you can't withdraw the bonus amount itself, only what you won while playing with it.

Concrete example

You deposit €100 with a 100% bonus: you get a €200 balance (€100 deposit + €100 bonus).

If you grow your balance to €500 by playing, you can withdraw the total minus the €100 offered by the casino, i.e. €400.

€500 balance − €100 bonus = €400 withdrawable

This is why casinos showing the "Wager free" badge on NoCapBonus are particularly appealing: no multiplier to reach, only the bonus amount itself stays non-withdrawable.

What about cashback with no wager?

This is exactly what makes cashback different from a classic bonus: it's usually paid with no betting condition. The money you receive is directly available, with no multiplier to reach.
